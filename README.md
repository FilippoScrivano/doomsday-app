A simple app to train your ability to guess the day of the week given any date in the calendar.
Features 3 game modes:
- Classic: just guess the day of the week given its date.
- 1900-2099: guess the day of the week given its date belonging to 20th or 21st centuries.
- 60s Blitz: guess as many days as you can in 60 seconds.

This game is entirely vibe-coded.

Enjoy!
